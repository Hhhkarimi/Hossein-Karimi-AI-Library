# Tool selection and procurement

Use this reference when MAP recommends tools or services or PROVE needs a vendor trial. Start with the workflow and incumbent entitlements. Specify inputs, output, language, existing systems, required controls, users, transaction volumes and the current process as a benchmark. Shortlist two or three viable candidates where available. Explain when only one survives.

## Discovery and purchase

These are starting points. Verify the exact SKU, listing, territory, terms and quote on the day of research.

| Need | Candidates to investigate | Where to verify or buy |
|---|---|---|
| Office copilots | Microsoft 365 Copilot or Gemini for Workspace where the organization already uses the relevant suite | [Microsoft enterprise plans](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing/enterprise), [Google Workspace plans](https://workspace.google.com/pricing.html) |
| Managed model API for a custom workflow | OpenAI API, Amazon Bedrock or Vertex AI | [OpenAI API pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing), [Bedrock pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/pricing/), [Vertex AI pricing](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/pricing) |
| SaaS, integrations and partner solutions | Look for ERP-native extensions and third-party products in the incumbent cloud ecosystem | [Microsoft Marketplace](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/marketplace), [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/), [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://cloud.google.com/marketplace) |
| Self-hosted model, OCR or classical ML | Evaluate model weights, OCR engines and ML libraries against the workload and license | [Hugging Face Models](https://huggingface.co/models) for discovery, original model card/repository for license, authorized cloud or hardware supplier for infrastructure |

For each shortlisted item record product, SKU, workflow fit and failure modes, official docs, exact listing or official quote channel, verification date, buyer country and deployment region, billing unit, currency, tax/fees, minimum commitment, plan dependencies and renewal terms. Mark sales-only prices as quote required. Mark access, policy or support gaps as conditional or blocked. Separate review directories and reseller leads from purchase channels. Verify reseller authorization, corporate invoicing, tenant ownership, warranty and support directly. For hardware compare exact part number, warranty, delivery, power and rack requirements against written supplier quotes.

For an Iran-based entity, verify the buyer's actual legal entity, supported territories, contractual eligibility, payment and billing, local-currency quote, data residency, connectivity and business continuity separately. Public listing or technical reachability alone establishes neither eligibility nor continuity. Obtain legal/procurement confirmation when uncertain. Exclude unsupported or unauthorized purchase paths and investigate compliant local, self-hosted or incumbent options.

## Qualification and selection

- Apply hard gates before scoring: lawful access, approved data handling, security requirements, integration feasibility and named owner. Compare feasible Buy, Build, Partner and Hybrid options plus the current baseline on a common scope, quality target, deployment conditions and cost horizon.
- Compare Persian quality on representative inputs, integration effort, latency and SLA, security controls, support, lock-in and TCO. Test with the same approved evaluation set for each candidate and use holdout cases for acceptance.
- For shortlisted offers ask for SSO/RBAC, audit trails, retention and training use, subprocessors, data export/deletion, incident notification, pricing overages, support SLA, exit costs and migration assistance. Ask for documented evidence and distinguish contract commitments from sales claims.
- Deliver a recommendation, viable fallback, rejected options and reasons, trial success threshold, budget ceiling, approving owner and next decision. Treat unresolved gates as conditions on any recommendation. Preparing a sourcing plan does not authorize purchasing or sending client data to vendors.
