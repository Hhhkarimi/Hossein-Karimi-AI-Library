# Style presets

## executive-tech
- White/off-white canvas
- Navy primary + cyan/teal accent
- Rounded cards with subtle border
- Sparse diagonal or rounded geometric motifs
- Outline icons with one accent fill
- Strong title hierarchy
- Best for AI, data, B2B, engineering, consulting

## editorial-minimal
- Typography-led
- Minimal icons
- Large margins and asymmetric whitespace
- Thin rules, quiet neutral palette
- Best for thought leadership, strategy, research

## bold-gradient
- Larger accent shapes
- Controlled gradients
- Punchier type scale
- Use for launches, creator/growth content
- Avoid for text-heavy legal/financial material

## luxury-data
- Deep navy/charcoal or warm ivory
- One restrained metallic/emerald accent
- Fine rules and premium spacing
- Best for finance, strategy, investment, executive content

## warm-educational
- Softer neutrals
- Friendly rounded icon containers
- Moderate color
- Best for tutorials, HR, learning, community content

## cyber-technical
- Dark background, grid/data motifs
- Electric cyan/purple accents
- High contrast
- Best for cybersecurity and deeply technical themes
- Keep copy density low
