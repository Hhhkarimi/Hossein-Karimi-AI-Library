# Palette strategy

## Roles

Every chosen palette should define these semantic roles:

- `background`: primary canvas
- `surface`: cards and chips
- `primary`: title / major shapes
- `accent`: emphasis / buttons / icon accent
- `text`: body copy
- `muted`: secondary text
- `border`: subtle separators

## Contrast targets

Aim for WCAG-like readability even though slides are images:

- body text: roughly 4.5:1 or better where possible
- large titles: roughly 3:1 or better
- never use cyan/light accent as body text on white

## Portrait-aware adjustment

If a portrait is used, inspect dominant clothing/background colors and avoid an accent that merges with the jacket or face. Preserve skin-tone separation from the background.
