# Synthetic worked examples
All data are invented for demonstration, not published research. Short examples demonstrate layout and fidelity, not default word length. Never reuse values in a real abstract.

## English observational example
Source facts: 120 urban grid cells; satellite observations; vegetation cover and land surface temperature; multivariable regression adjusted for building density; negative association, no supplied effect size; observational design. No causal inference or invented coefficients.

Abstract

Urban vegetation may moderate heat exposure, but the magnitude of this relationship varies across urban landscapes. This synthetic study examined associations between vegetation cover and land surface temperature in 120 urban grid cells using satellite observations and multivariable regression. Greater vegetation cover was associated with lower land surface temperature after adjustment for building density. The observational design does not establish causality. These findings illustrate how remotely sensed indicators can inform the assessment of urban thermal environments.

Keywords: Urban heat island; Urban vegetation; Land surface temperature; Remote sensing; Urban climate

## French theoretical example
Source facts: an article argues that algorithmic accountability requires both procedural explanation and institutional review; develops a conceptual framework by comparing two normative approaches; no empirical data. Appropriate output:

Résumé

Cet article examine les conditions de la responsabilité algorithmique en confrontant deux approches normatives. Il défend l'idée que l'explication des procédures de décision doit être complétée par un contrôle institutionnel et propose un cadre conceptuel articulant ces deux exigences. Cette perspective permet de distinguer la compréhension des décisions de la capacité à en contester les effets, sans prétendre démontrer empiriquement l'efficacité du cadre proposé.

Mots-clés : responsabilité algorithmique; transparence algorithmique; contrôle institutionnel; gouvernance; cadre normatif
