# Quality assurance rubric
Apply silently before delivery. This is an editorial heuristic, not a validated scientific assessment instrument.

## Hard failures (revise or disclose a blocker)
- Invented or unsupported empirical result, numeric claim, study design, registration, novelty or citation.
- Material conflict with the source; misleading causal statement; suppressed primary outcome.
- Wrong language or paragraph structure; violated explicit hard word/keyword count.
- Irrelevant keyword, keyword stuffing, missing keyword line (unless not requested).
- Unread manuscript presented as read; incomplete research presented as completed.

## Review dimensions (0 deficient / 1 partial / 2 adequate)
1. Problem and objective alignment; 2. appropriate methodology detail; 3. primary findings or arguments; 4. uncertainty and scope; 5. conclusion fidelity; 6. coherence and information density; 7. disciplinary and linguistic precision; 8. keyword relevance and complementarity.
Mark truly inapplicable dimensions N/A. Aim for 2 on each applicable dimension, not an arbitrary acceptance threshold. Do not show scores unless requested. A validator cannot assess these semantic dimensions by itself.

## Final pass
Trace each material clause to source; recheck quantities, signs, units, denominators and comparator; distinguish absence of evidence from evidence of absence; ensure every keyword is supported; verify count and formatting. Revise until hard failures are resolved or transparently explain what prevents completion.
