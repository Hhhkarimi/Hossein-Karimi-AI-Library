# Scientific Sources

## Reference guidance (consult only when needed)

These references inform reporting and discoverability practices; they do not override the user's format preferences or authorize introducing external claims into an abstract.

- ICMJE, Preparing a Manuscript for Submission to a Medical Journal: https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/manuscript-preparation/preparing-for-submission.html
- APA Journal Article Reporting Standards: https://apastyle.apa.org/jars
- EQUATOR Network reporting guidelines: https://www.equator-network.org/
- PRISMA for Abstracts: https://www.prisma-statement.org/abstracts
- CONSORT: https://www.consort-spirit.org/
- Wiley, SEO guidance for journal authors: https://www.wiley.com/publishing/article/authors/author-resources/seo-guide-to-enhance-article-visibility
- SAGE, Help readers find your article: https://www.sagepub.com/journals/information-for-authors/preparing-your-manuscript/help-readers-find-your-article
- Title, abstract and keywords: a practical guide to maximize the visibility and impact of academic papers (2024): https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2024.1222
